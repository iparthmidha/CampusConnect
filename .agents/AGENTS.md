# Campus Connect — Design System & Institutional Guidelines

Product: Campus Connect — AI-powered Faculty–Student Coordination Platform
For: Sri Guru Gobind Singh College of Commerce (SGGSCC)

## Design style
- Modern Academic Digital Infrastructure
- Professional, clean, institutional, calm, trustworthy
- Minimalist, high contrast, mature
- Avoid AI SaaS tropes: zero neon glows, futuristic AI visuals, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, or fake dashboard mockups on public pages
- Prioritize usability, legibility, and accessibility

## Color Palette (Light & Dark Theme)
- **Primary**: Black (`#000000` / `#09090b` / `zinc-950`) in Light Mode, White (`#ffffff` / `zinc-50`) in Dark Mode
- **Secondary**: Dark Charcoal (`#18181b` / `zinc-900`), Cool Light Gray (`#f4f4f5` / `zinc-100`)
- **Background**:
  - Light Mode: Off-white / White (`#ffffff` / `#fafafa` / `zinc-50`)
  - Dark Mode: Deep Charcoal / Black (`#09090b` / `#121215` / `zinc-950`)
- **Text**:
  - Light Mode: Black / Charcoal (`#09090b` / `#27272a`)
  - Dark Mode: Off-white / Light Gray (`#fafafa` / `#e4e4e7`)
- **Accents**: Muted gray borders, subtle college identity hints

## Typography
- Modern sans-serif (Inter / system sans)
- Clear hierarchy: large page titles, medium section headers, legible supporting body text
- Maximum 2 font families

## UI Components
- Rounded corners (`rounded-lg`, `rounded-xl`)
- Consistent spacing, clean subtle borders (`border-zinc-200` in light, `border-zinc-800` in dark)
- shadcn/ui components & Lucide icons
- Light/Dark mode toggle available globally

## Navigation & Page Structure
- Public pages introduce the platform without showing authenticated dashboard mockups or fake task data
- Consistent navbar & institutional footer across all pages
- Reusable components only; keep design language strictly unified
